Assessment

How will you be assessed?

In this project, your work will be assessed in two main ways.

First, your final product will be evaluated using a rubric. This means that your campaign will be assessed according to clear criteria such as content, language use, organisation, creativity and use of digital tools. The rubric helps you understand what is expected at each level and shows you how you can improve your work step by step.

Second, you will use a self-assessment target (or assessment wheel). This tool helps you reflect on your own learning after speaking activities. You will mark your level in different areas such as fluency, accuracy, the structures you use, etc. This will help you become more aware of your progress and identify what you need to practise more in future tasks.
