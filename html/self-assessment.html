Self-assessment

What do I use this for?

This assessment tool helps you reflect on your own learning. It allows you to evaluate different aspects of a task, such as your fluency, accuracy, participation or confidence, by marking your level in each area. This helps you become more aware of your strengths and the areas you need to improve, and it encourages you to take an active role in your learning process.
