Rural Depopulation as a Global Phenomenon

C1 UNIT

Details of the project

Details

Title of the Project: Rural Depopulation as a Global Phenomenon

Topic: This project focuses on the global phenomenon of depopulation, exploring its causes (migration to cities, lack of opportunities, etc.), its social and economic consequences, and possible solutions. It also examines how this phenomenon affects countries such as the United Kingdom, Ireland, and the United States.

Level: C1

Estimated Duration: Between 4 and 5 sessions.

Language: English.

Learning Outcomes: The aim of this project is to help you develop both your linguistic competence and your critical understanding of a relevant global issue. The project seeks to provide opportunities for analysis and reflection.

Throughout the project, you will:

- Analyse a variety of authentic materials (articles, videos, infographics) related to rural depopulation in different parts of the world.
- Expand their advanced vocabulary and collocations (e.g. rural exodus, demographic imbalance, infrastructure gap, sustainable development).
- Develop critical-thinking skills by comparing different contexts and evaluating policy responses.
- Practise advanced speaking and writing skills, including debating, argumentation, and the production of well-structured texts.
