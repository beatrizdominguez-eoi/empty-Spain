Rural Depopulation as a Global Phenomenon

C1 UNIT

Digital tools you will use

Digital tools

For this project, you will use a variety of digital tools to research information and create your final awareness-raising campaign on rural depopulation.

You will work collaboratively using Google Docs and Google Slides to share ideas, organise information, and prepare your content. You may use Canva or Genially to design an interactive infographic or digital presentation, combining text, images, videos, and other resources to communicate your message effectively.

You can also use copyright-free image banks such as Pixabay to find suitable visual materials and learn how to use online resources responsibly.

By completing this project, you will not only improve your English language skills but also develop your digital competence, creativity, teamwork, and ability to work independently.
